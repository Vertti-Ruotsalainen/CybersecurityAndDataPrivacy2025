# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 18.12.2025

Booking System values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

- **Email address** – used to identify your account and display your bookings.
- **Date of birth** – used to verify age eligibility.
- **IP address and usage logs** – automatically collected for security, troubleshooting, and service improvement purposes.

---

## 2. Why We Collect Your Data

Your information is used to:

- Verify compliance with age requirements.
- Display and manage your bookings within your account.
- Maintain security, prevent fraud, and ensure the proper operation of the application.

---

## 3. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to provide our services or until you request its deletion.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. Your information may be shared with trusted service providers (such as email services) who assist in operating the app and are required to comply with strict data protection standards.

- Request a copy of your data in a portable format.
